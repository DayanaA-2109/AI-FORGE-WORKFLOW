Smallholder fruit and vegetable farmers in Nashik, Maharashtra, experience significant post-harvest losses and reduced net income because they lack affordable first-mile cooling, grading facilities, and refrigerated transportation, while depending on middlemen for immediate credit.

Current approaches, such as individual cold-storage facilities, traditional intermediaries, and separate agricultural credit services, often fail to provide an integrated, affordable solution that preserves produce quality while maintaining farmers’ access to finance.
