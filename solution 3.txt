We will build KisanDecision, a web tool that takes a Bihar tomato farmer’s district, quantity, harvest time and local temperature and, using the latest same-day Agmarknet mandi prices (data.gov.in), a transparent heat-based spoilage model and estimated transport cost, calculates net revenue for sell locally, move to a nearby mandi, or hold. A language model explains the result in plain language. It needs no cold-chain hardware, only a phone and an internet connection, and runs on free APIs and free hosting
