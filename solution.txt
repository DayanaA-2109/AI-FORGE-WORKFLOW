
FarmBridge AI is a decision-support tool for smallholder tomato farmers in Patna, Bihar. It helps them decide when, where, and how to sell their harvest before it spoils — and how to afford getting it there.

The core idea
Most price tools just show mandi rates. FarmBridge goes further: it combines spoilage risk + net market returns + shared transport + micro-credit into one practical recommendation.

How it works — four layers
1. Spoilage-Risk Layer
Uses live weather, harvest timing, and reported tomato condition to estimate how fast the produce will deteriorate. Flags batches that need urgent action.

2. Market Decision Engine
Compares tomato prices across two markets and calculates net returns after transport cost and expected quality loss — not just the headline price.

3. Group Harvest Rescue
Farmers pool their small quantities into one shipment. This cuts per-farmer transport cost and makes cold transport viable for smallholders who couldn't afford it alone.

4. Embedded Micro-Credit Estimate
Calculates the funding gap for transport and shows a simulated short-term credit option. Real lending would need a verified partner and consent.

The main innovation
Group Harvest Rescue + Cold-Transport Financing. Instead of just telling farmers "Market A pays more," it helps them coordinate a shared shipment and figure out how to pay for it — solving the two biggest blockers at once.
